About Starview

Every skyline begins
With a spark

Mangalam Starview is a thoughtfully planned residential project by Mangalam Landmarks, located near Mukai Chowk, Kiwale, Pune.

Spread across a 6-acre land parcel, the project features four residential towers rising 27 storeys, built using advanced Mivan construction technology.

Spacious 2 & 3 BHK residences, contemporary architecture, landscaped open spaces and lifestyle amenities come together to create a comfortable modern living environment.
